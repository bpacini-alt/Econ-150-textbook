# Chapter 16: current-revision Precise Practice integration

This clean pilot is based on the **latest main Chapter 16**, which now has **four major concepts**, not the six in the superseded PR #100. The entire current chapter prose and its existing harbor-town FRQ are preserved.

## Checkpoints (in chapter order)

1. Markets Are Built — institutions supporting exchange, open entry, enforcement.
2. Property Rights — use, transfer, exclusion, credible claims.
3. Corruption — inclusive/extractive institutions; **transaction costs remain within this concept**, with relevant former transaction-cost Tier 2 cases retained as additional questions.
4. Public Goods and Common Resources — rivalry/excludability, common-resource problems, free riders.

At each checkpoint: Tier 1 rapid foundational practice, flashcards, Rapid Sort, Definition Detective, Missing Piece; Tier 2 applied case questions plus Find & Fix the Error; Tier 3 explanation prompts with self-checks.

The final concept checkpoint leads to an optional **four-part chapter challenge**, a new scenario about a farm exchange and cold-storage cooperative, deliberately distinct from the chapter's existing harbor-town FRQ. This is self-review, not automatic FRQ scoring or an official test-out.

## Design principles and limitations

- Student can open any tier or skip straight to chapter-level synthesis; no forced paths or minimum question count.
- Engine remains in a standalone reusable asset `etb-live/assets/chapter16-precise-practice.html`; checkpoints use same-origin iframe shells.
- Local progress/drafts only, no server or gradebook integration. A vertical bar tracks distinct prompts attempted, not mastery. Written Tier 3 answers are not graded.
- We **did not carry over** the old separate Transaction Costs and The Rights You Do Not Have checkpoints, as those are absent from the current four-concept structure. Some transaction-cost practice is retained where the concept and current FRQ support it.
- Validation still required: full browser test, mobile/accessibility, instructional review of distractors and free-response rubrics, iframe resizing, and potential duplicate assignment burden. PR #100 is an older, outdated pilot and should **not** be merged over this current chapter.
