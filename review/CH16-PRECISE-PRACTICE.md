# Chapter 16 Precise Practice pilot

Standalone **review-only** HTML prototype at [chapter-16-precise-practice.html](chapter-16-precise-practice.html), without modifying the live chapter.

- Six concept checkpoints drawn from Chapter 16: institutions, property rights, corruption, transaction costs, goods classification, and rights versus objections.
- For each: 10–12 rapid Tier 1 questions plus four flip-card terms; five Tier 2 application questions; two Tier 3 open-response prompts.
- Chapter challenge: six integrated parts concerning lake management, corresponding to all six concepts.
- All concepts and tiers are freely selectable. The challenge can be attempted first. No forced grinding.
- Browser-only draft/progress persistence. Progress bar reflects distinct attempts, **not** mastery; understanding status is provisional.
- Written Tier 3 and chapter responses are self-check only, not automatically graded or qualifying for formal test-out.
- Production tasks: better error-sensitive retries and shuffled variants, diverse interaction types, richer scoring rubrics, QA, accessibility review, and actual placement in the chapter.
