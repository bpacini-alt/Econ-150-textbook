# Chapter 16 exam practice selection update

- Chapter-picker uses native **square multiple-selection checkboxes** inside collapsible **Units 1–4**, with chapter groupings sourced from the current Econ table of contents. Unit 2 shows Chapter 14, Unit 3 shows Chapters 15–16. Only Chapter 16 has a connected reviewed-for-pilot bank; 14/15 are displayed disabled rather than deceptively selectable.
- Length choices: **5, 10 (default, matching quiz length), 20, or Keep practicing**.
- A student can continue past a chosen session length rather than stopping at a hard endpoint.
- The Chapter 16 bank currently contains **12 distinct exam-level draft questions**. A 20-question or open-ended session necessarily cycles those questions, and the UI states that transparently. New banks expand the pool later.
- No adaptive scheduling, scoring or mastery judgments have been introduced. Existing concept checkpoint navigation stays intact.
- Unit accordion expansion is visual-only; a future multi-bank loader will allow genuinely selecting any combination of reviewed chapters.
