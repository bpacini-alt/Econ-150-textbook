# Chapter 16 prototype — checkpoint accordions, suggested practice, and spiral controls (2026-10-31)

Implemented in PR #112:
- Four checkpoint panels are now native collapsed accordions within the canonical chapter preview. Reading remains uncluttered until the student elects to practice.
- Per-tier tracker targets: Tier 1 **10**, Tier 2 **5**, Tier 3 **3** attempts. Counters advance when a student actually checks a computer-scored problem or classification board. These arbitrary prototype thresholds mean **"you can move on if you feel ready"**, not mastery or an assignment requirement. Bar caps at 100%, practice continues indefinitely.
- End-of-chapter spiral selector shows earlier chapters ordered by **Unit then Chapter** (Unit 2: Chapter 14; Unit 3: Chapter 15). Both begin selected. Students can narrow selected earlier chapters; the current Chapter 16 remains included.
- The mixed final readiness check continues to require at least one prior chapter and five consecutive correct-and-understood answers before the original Harbor Town FRQ unlocks.
- The chapter's existing FRQ markup and authorial prose are preserved.

Research/evaluation questions for students and instructor:
1. Do the 10/5/3 markers encourage voluntary useful practice or mindless completion? Does counting attempts rather than distinct meaningful examples feel wrong?
2. Does accordioning reduce cognitive load without hiding something students need?
3. Is default-all spiral review welcome, and can students easily locate and narrow their chapters?
4. Do students understand this is a practice recommendation, not an official grade or validated mastery estimate?

Known limits: The prior banks are small draft samples, the concept checkpoint formats have uneven item variation, and embedded browser/mobile/accessibility tests remain outstanding. Expand chapter inclusion only after approved banks exist; don't treat this prototype as final release.
