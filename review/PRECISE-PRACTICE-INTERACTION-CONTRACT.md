# Precise Practice four-tier and interaction contract — October 31, 2026

Applies to Econ and EDCOR chapter prototypes.

1. All displayed answer alternatives MUST be shuffled per question attempt. Keep order stable while student answers and reviews feedback, preserve the canonical answer key and map visible positions back to that key. Test across multiple attempts for patterns. Strong distractors still matter.
2. Tier 1: foundations/definitions and intuition. Tier 2: supported application and prediction. Tier 3: difficult AP-style selected-response reasoning that is computer scorable. No essays, writing boxes, or unscored FRQs at Tier 3.
3. Tier 4: constructed responses (Econ FRQ; EDCOR AAQ-inspired Research Detective, EBQ-inspired Evidence-Based Decision, and Classroom Judgment), offered only after chapter-end test-out.
4. Prefer hands-on drag and drop for classification and sorting, **with equivalent keyboard/touch/screen reader fallback**. Students must be able to complete the exercise without dragging.
5. Concept checkpoints remain collapsed and support blocked format, mixed formats, and mixing tiers. End-of-chapter test-out interleaves current and prior chapters, requiring five consecutive correct answers plus self-reported understanding before opening Tier 4.
6. Never treat a five-item streak as measured mastery or evidence of an official grade. Ask whether students could explain why answers are correct, then after the model FRQ prompt whether they need more practice.
7. Pilot verification: validate shuffled keys, score and feedback after multiple shuffles, drag/drop, keyboard classification, narrow screen, Tier 3 non-text interaction, and reading/answer feedback for correctness.
