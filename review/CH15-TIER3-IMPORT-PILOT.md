# Chapter 15 Tier 3 Import Pilot

This pilot imports the historical Elasticity practice quiz into the current Econ precise-practice engine and places the practice at the end of the relevant Chapter 15 concept.

## Source

- Structured source bank: https://github.com/bpacini-alt/practice-engines/blob/pilot/econ-ch15-elasticity-json/items/econ-150/ch15-elasticity-practice.json
- Original practice quiz and answer key are preserved in the practice-engines legacy archive.
- The imported bank contains 68 questions in four original pools. The textbook pilot exposes them through the engine's Tier 3/AP-style multiple-choice mode.
- The pilot maps the historical items to the chapter's seven concepts: 20, 4, 11, 2, 14, 17, 0 items respectively.

## Concept placement

- Price Elasticity of Demand: 20 items
- Determinants of Elasticity: 4 items
- The Total Revenue Test: 11 items
- Tax Incidence: 2 items
- Price Elasticity of Supply: 14 items
- Cross-Price and Income Elasticities: 17 items
- The Limits of Efficiency: no historical item cleanly fits yet

The bank's original source groups do not line up perfectly with the textbook's concept boundaries, so these are provisional editorial mappings. Tax incidence is intentionally thin, and the efficiency section remains an explicit gap rather than receiving unrelated questions.

## Deliberate pilot status

Every item remains `legacy-unreviewed`. Treating the bank as Tier 3 here is an interface and learning-experience test, not a final difficulty classification. Tier 1 and Tier 2 are intentionally blank in this pilot.

The pilot should help us see:

- whether practice appears at the right point in the chapter;
- whether the question presentation and answer randomization work;
- whether the feedback is useful enough;
- which legacy items actually deserve Tier 3 status;
- which items need diagrams, revision, or removal; and
- whether the five-question readiness bar feels sensible.

Questions 8, 9, 12, and 15 reference diagrams that were not available in the readable text extraction and need visual recovery before final use.

The pilot does not alter the chapter's FRQ or completion rules.
