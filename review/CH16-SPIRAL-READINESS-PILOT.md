# Chapter 16 prototype — spiral readiness and metacognition (2026-10-31)

- Concept checkpoints remain independent; the original Harbor Town FRQ markup is **unchanged**, now behind a readiness gate.
- Final check: select earlier chapter bank(s) (14 Poverty and Incentives, 15 Elasticity), then complete **three current-chapter and two earlier-chapter** items in a five-consecutive-correct-and-understood sequence. The student confirms they can explain each correct answer; incorrect or uncertain answers reset the streak.
- On completion the original FRQ unlocks; after attempting and comparing with its original scoring guide, students choose “I can explain it” or “I need more practice.” The final message does not claim grade submission or mastery.
- Earlier-chapter questions are **small, newly authored pilot banks**, not yet reviewed or approved by an instructor; they are sourced to real topics in those chapters. The shared engine can draw from fuller banks when available.
- Reusable logic lives in `etb-live/assets/precise-readiness-pilot.js`. No AI feedback, no persistent personal analytics, no adaptive mastery claims.
- Browser and accessibility testing, student usability review, and expert item validation remain required. PR #112 is still a pilot.
