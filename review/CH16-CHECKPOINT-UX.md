# Chapter 16 checkpoint UX consolidation

- **One practice surface only**: the original quick checks and the added formats are now modes of the same interface, not stacked boxes.
- **Mix it up** is the default. It rotates through rapid classifications, six-item sorting, Definition Detective, Missing Piece and flipcards in Tier 1, and applied cases/error repair in Tier 2. Students may choose any specific practice style from a single selector. Tier 3 remains independent reasoning.
- Each style has a concise instruction and consistent Check / Next controls; no “new” or “extra practice” copy is exposed to students.
- Child iframe reports the height of its *actual content* rather than its own iframe viewport; parent frames can shrink instead of remaining long and empty. Smaller fallback frame heights are used before measurement.
- Still review-only and provisional. Further refinements: per-skill adaptive selection, richer content banks, calibrated evidence indicator from all formats, mobile/browser checks and keyboard/screen-reader review.
