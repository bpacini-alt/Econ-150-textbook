## Status definitions

- **Unverified:** No full chapter audit is recorded. By default, leave current wording in place and assume it is acceptable while review is pending.
- **Needs review:** A known wording, source, count, or placement decision remains open; follow the note in that row and the detailed candidate-bank entry.
- **Approved:** Benjamin has reviewed the chapter's aphorism choices, exact wording and placement, and the implementation is merged. No chapter currently meets this full-audit definition.

During each chapter review, count existing cards first, verify sources and context, compare the full chapter with candidates, and record whether a second or optional third line adds value. If a chapter cannot support a second strong line, record the exception for Benjamin rather than adding a weak aphorism.
