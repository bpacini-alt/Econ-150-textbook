Question 1 · Methodological Commitments

An economist claims: "The adoption of open trade policies and structured property rights in South Korea after 1953 caused its long-term output per worker to vastly exceed that of North Korea." How should this statement be classified?

✓ Correct! This is a positive statement . It makes an objective claim about cause and effect ("System X caused Outcome Y") that can be tested by tracking actual economic history and productivity data, completely separate from your personal political or moral opinions.
✗ Incorrect. Look carefully at the wording. The claim doesn't use subjective words like "ought," "should," "just," or "unfair." It asserts a structural fact that can be checked using satellite data and GDP records. Review your Chapter 1 flashcards for the difference between positive and normative analysis.
