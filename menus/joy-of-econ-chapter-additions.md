# Joy of Econ chapter-addition backlog

This is an internal editorial backlog, not part of the student-facing Joy of Econ menu.

These readings were removed from the menu because they largely reinforce concepts already covered in the textbook and therefore should not count toward the Joy requirement. When the relevant chapter is revised, evaluate each item as **add**, **companion**, or **discard**. Prefer a compact, useful placement—such as a “Further exploration” link or a short callout—over adding another required assignment.
