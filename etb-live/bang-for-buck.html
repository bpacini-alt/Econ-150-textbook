Chetty: Visualizing the American Dream

Watch the reading ↗

Does your zip code decide how far you'll rise?

Raj Chetty's deep dive, built on anonymized tax records for tens of millions of Americans: how the neighborhood a child grows up in — sometimes down to the block — along with the quality of their teachers and the college they attend shapes their odds of out-earning their parents (now roughly a coin flip, down from ~90% mid-century). A plutocrat's case against trickle-down.

Nick Hanauer — a self-described plutocrat — argues that decades of trickle-down orthodoxy got the economy backwards: workers and their spending, not the wealthy, are the real engine of growth, which makes rising inequality a threat rather than a harmless byproduct. A pointed demand-side critique of the standard story, and a deliberate sparring partner for the market-friendly voices elsewhere in the course.

TED talk (~18 min) — watch the whole thing.
