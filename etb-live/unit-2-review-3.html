Idea 3

Profit is a flare

In the short run, profit keeps score. In the long run, it sends a signal to everyone outside the industry.

Economic profit → resources would earn more here → new firms enter → market supply shifts right → price falls → profit shrinks toward zero

Losses work the other way: firms exit until the losses are gone. When economic profit is zero, firms earn a normal profit. They do as well as their next-best option, so no one has a reason to enter or leave.

Accountants are good people; they answer a different question.

An accountant asks whether more money came in than went out. An economist asks whether this beat the next-best use of the owner's time and money. So economic profit also subtracts what the owner gave up.
